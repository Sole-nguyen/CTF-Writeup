🚀 Game Rules & Controls

🎯 Objective

Defend Earth from the alien invasion! Destroy as many aliens as possible before they reach the bottom of the screen. Each alien destroyed gives you 10 points.

🎮 Controls

← → Move spaceship left/right
SPACE Fire laser bullets
P Pause/Resume game

👽 Enemy Types

Scout
Small, fast, 10 points
Fighter
Medium, crab-like, 10 points
Bomber
Large, slow, 10 points

⚡ Game Features

  • 3 lives to start with
  • Difficulty increases with your score
