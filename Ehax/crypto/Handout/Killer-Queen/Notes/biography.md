Pafnuti Lwowitsch Tschebyschow
Пафну́тий Льво́вич Чебышёв
Born: May 16, 1821, Okatovo, Russia
Died: December 8, 1894, Saint Petersburg

Known for:
  • Chebyshev polynomials
  • Chebyshev's inequality
  • Chebyshev's theorem (prime distribution)
  • Approximation theory

His polynomials appear in:
  • Signal processing (minimax approximation)
  • Numerical analysis (Clenshaw-Curtis quadrature)
  • Cryptography (Chebyshev-based key exchange)

The composition identity  T_m(T_n(x)) = T_{mn}(x)
makes them a commutative semigroup under composition,
analogous to exponentiation in multiplicative groups.

This creates a one-way function:
  Given x and T_n(x) mod p, find n.

The discrete logarithm problem in the Chebyshev setting
reduces — via the Dickson polynomial correspondence —
to the standard DLP in (Z/pZ)*.
