Perhaps you thought of elliptic curves?
A reasonable guess for a queen of such refinement.

But her curve has no y-coordinate.
Her points live on a line, not a plane.

The group law is simpler than you think —
yet the logarithm remains just as stubborn.

---

Think instead of a sequence.
Two initial values.
A recurrence that folds back on itself.

The composition of two such sequences
is itself a sequence of the same kind —
indexed by the product.
