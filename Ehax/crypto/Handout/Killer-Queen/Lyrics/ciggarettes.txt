To avoid complications
She never kept the same secret twice
In conversation

She spoke just like a function —
and never changed her answer
for the same question asked again.

What she offers with one hand,
she transforms with the other.

Both voices sing the same refrain,
but one begins where the other left off.

-----------------------------
If you listen to the first voice
and then ask the second
to repeat the first voice's song —
you will hear a composition.
-----------------------------
